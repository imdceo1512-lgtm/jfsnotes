# K — Senior DBA scenarios and capstone

- [K-01 Incident practicum: log full, locks, storage, and connectivity](K-01.md) — Use this lesson to make a bounded decision about log-full evidence and containment, blocking transaction and rollback risk, filesystem/container/log capacity. Confirm target, authority, state, and recovery boundary before changing anything.
- [K-02 Recovery practicum: full, incremental, point-in-time, and tablespace recovery](K-02.md) — Use this lesson to make a bounded decision about full image and isolated restore, automatic incremental chain, manual incremental chain. Confirm target, authority, state, and recovery boundary before changing anything.
- [K-03 Availability/change practicum: HADR, upgrade, failover, and rollback decisions](K-03.md) — Use this lesson to make a bounded decision about HADR role and synchronization, planned takeover and fencing, forced takeover and data-loss gate. Confirm target, authority, state, and recovery boundary before changing anything.
- [K-04 Enterprise capstone: architecture, security, recovery, performance, and operations](K-04.md) — Use this lesson to make a bounded decision about workload objectives and SLO, topology and independent failure domains, HADR/replication role and client route. Confirm target, authority, state, and recovery boundary before changing anything.
